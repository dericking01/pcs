Account and Data Deletion Request

App Name: infoma
Developer: Pannecia Apps
Last Updated: July 25, 2026

We respect your right to privacy and control over your personal data. If you wish to delete your infoma account and associated data, please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended & Fastest)

  1. Open the infoma app on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to Settings (usually found in your Profile menu).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap on "Delete Account".
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your decision. Your account will be scheduled for immediate deletion.

Method 2: Email Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion via email:

  1. Send an email to info@pcs.co.tz.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Send the email from the exact email address associated with your infoma account.
  4. Include your full name and the phone number (if applicable) linked to the account to help us verify your identity.

Our support team will process your request within 3 to 5 business days and send you a confirmation email once completed.

What Happens to Your Data?

Upon confirmation of your deletion request, we take the following actions regarding your data:

✅ Data Deleted Immediately (Within 7 Days)

  • Your account profile (name, email address, phone number).
  • All authentication tokens and login credentials.
  • Your personal scan history, monitored accounts, and custom security preferences.
  • Any active push notification subscriptions linked to your device.

⚠️ Data Retained for Legal & Compliance Reasons

Please note that certain data must be retained for a limited period to comply with financial regulations, fraud prevention, and our Terms of Service:

  • Transaction Records: Payment receipts and subscription logs are retained for up to 7 years as required by financial auditing and tax laws. This data is strictly anonymized and cannot be linked back to your personal profile.
  • Anonymized Analytics: Aggregated, non-identifiable usage data (e.g., "100 users scanned a URL today") is kept indefinitely to improve app security features.
  • Security Logs: Logs related to confirmed malicious activity or fraud may be retained for up to 90 days to protect our systems and other users.

Reactivation and Cancellation

Once your account deletion process is complete, it is permanent and cannot be undone. You will lose access to all premium features and historical data. If you wish to use infoma again in the future, you will need to create a brand new account.

If you only wish to cancel your paid subscription but keep your free account, please use the "Cancel Subscription" option in the app or contact your payment provider (DPO Group) directly, rather than deleting your account.
